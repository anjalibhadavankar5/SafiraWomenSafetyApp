Self Defence Tips

Knowledge is your first weapon. Stay informed, stay safe.

01
Awareness

Stay Alert in Public

Keep your head up and stay aware of your surroundings. Avoid using your phone while walking alone, especially at night. Attackers often target distracted individuals.

02
Awareness

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong, it probably is. Don't ignore that inner feeling. Move to a crowded, well-lit place immediately and call someone you trust.

03
Physical

Target Vulnerable Points

In an attack, strike the eyes, nose, throat, or groin with full force. These are the most sensitive areas and can disable an attacker long enough for you to escape.

04
Physical

Use Your Elbows & Knees

Elbows and knees are your strongest natural weapons. At close range, a sharp elbow strike to the face or knee to the groin is extremely effective. Practice in self-defence class.

05
Physical

Break a Wrist Grab

If someone grabs your wrist, rotate your arm toward the attacker's thumb — the weakest point of their grip — and pull sharply. This almost always breaks the hold.

06
Physical

Make Noise & Run

Scream "FIRE!" (people respond faster to fire than assault). Make as much noise as possible and run toward the nearest crowded, well-lit area. Your goal is escape, not fighting.

07
Awareness

Safe Travel at Night

Share your live location with a trusted contact. Prefer well-lit, busy roads. Sit near the driver in autos/taxis. Verify OLA/Uber driver details before getting in.

08
Digital Safety

Protect Your Privacy Online

Never share your real-time location publicly on social media. Set all accounts to private. Be cautious of strangers asking for personal information online.

09
Digital Safety

Beware of Stalkerware

Check your phone for unknown apps that track your location. Signs include battery draining fast, phone getting hot, or unusual data usage. Use trusted security apps.

10
Legal Rights

Section 354 IPC — Your Protection

Any man who assaults or uses criminal force on a woman intending to outrage her modesty can be imprisoned for up to 5 years. Know your rights — report immediately.

11
Legal Rights

Zero FIR — File Anywhere

You can file a Zero FIR at ANY police station regardless of jurisdiction. The police cannot refuse to register your complaint. Demand it if they refuse.

12
Legal Rights

Right to Free Legal Aid

Every woman who is a victim of crime has the right to free legal aid under the Legal Services Authorities Act. Contact your nearest District Legal Services Authority.
